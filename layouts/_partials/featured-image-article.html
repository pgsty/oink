{{- /* Render a blog article's featured image, as a banner or as a wash. `featured-image-resolve.html` owns which image a page carries and the URL policy that goes with it, so what a reader sees at the top of an article and what its social card carries cannot drift apart. `featured-image-mode.html` owns whether this partial runs: by the time it does, the page has an image and the mode is one of the two rendered here. A banner is a figure above the title. A processable local raster is cropped to one fixed 16:9 frame so a run of articles keeps a single rhythm; SVG, a static path, and a remote URL are framed by CSS in that same box instead, because Hugo cannot process them. Its `alt` is empty on purpose: a banner restates the title it sits under, and the title is its accessible name. An image that carries information of its own belongs in the body, where it can be described. It is never lazy: a banner is the page's largest paintable element. A wash is decorative by construction. It sits behind the article header at a low opacity, is hidden from assistive technology, and is resized down first -- nothing of the detail survives the opacity, so the bytes are waste. Input: dict "page" "mode" "banner" | "wash". */ -}} {{- $page := .page -}} {{- $found := partialCached "featured-image-resolve.html" $page $page.RelPermalink -}} {{- $src := $found.href -}} {{- $byline := "" -}} {{- with $found.resource -}} {{- $src = .RelPermalink -}} {{- if reflect.IsImageResourceProcessable . -}} {{- if eq $.mode "banner" -}} {{- $src = (.Fill "1600x900 Center").RelPermalink -}} {{- else -}} {{- $src = (.Resize "1200x q60").RelPermalink -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- $byline = .Params.byline | default "" -}} {{- end -}} {{- if eq .mode "banner" -}} {{- else -}}
