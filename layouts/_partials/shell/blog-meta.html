{{- /* The one line of metadata under a post's title in a list, a card, or a term archive: when it was published, who wrote it, and where it lives. Three call sites rendered their own version of this line and drifted -- two of them appended the byline after the section instead of inside the sentence, and the card carried no byline at all. They all come here now. The whole line is one translated sentence rather than fragments joined in the template, because "in" is not a word every language puts between the same two things, and a byline is not a suffix in every language either: English 2026-08-08, By OINK maintainers, In Oink Chinese 2026-08-08，由 OINK 维护者 发布于 Oink A post with no `authors` term -- including one that still carries the 0.4 `author:` string -- takes the shorter sentence. A page with no date at all gets no line: the sentence opens with the date in both languages, and the row form already suppressed date and section together for that case. Authors are joined with `ui_list_separator` because a sentence needs punctuation between names; the article byline still spaces them with CSS gap, where it can. A card passes `authors false`: it has room for the date, the section, the tags, and three lines of description, and the byline is the first thing that loses the argument for that space. The article and the row still name who wrote the post. Input: dict "page" ["authors" false]. Returns markup, or nothing when the page has neither a date nor a section to name. */ -}} {{- $page := .page -}} {{- $withAuthors := ne .authors false -}} {{- $authors := cond $withAuthors (partial "authors-resolve.html" $page) slice -}} {{- $names := slice -}} {{- range $authors -}} {{- $names = $names | append (printf `%s` (.url | htmlEscape) (.name | htmlEscape)) -}} {{- end -}} {{- $section := "" -}} {{- with $page.CurrentSection -}} {{- $section = printf `%s` (.RelPermalink | htmlEscape) (.LinkTitle | htmlEscape) -}} {{- end -}} {{- $date := "" -}} {{- if not $page.Date.IsZero -}} {{- $date = printf `%s` ($page.Date.Format "2006-01-02") ($page.Date.Format ($page.Param "time_format_blog")) -}} {{- end -}} {{- if and $date $section -}} {{- $context := dict "Date" $date "Authors" (delimit $names (T "ui_list_separator")) "Section" $section -}}

{{- if $names }}{{ T "post_meta_by_in" $context | safeHTML }}{{ else }}{{ T "post_meta_in" $context | safeHTML }}{{ end -}}

{{- end -}}
