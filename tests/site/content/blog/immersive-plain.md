An empty image list turns off what the blog cascade carries, so the hero mode
resolves and then renders nothing: no backdrop, no pushed-down opening, and
the navbar back to its ordinary sticky self. The other keys hold on their own:
no sidebar, no clouds, a flow rail — and no breadcrumb, which is the blog
shell's own default rather than part of the recipe.

## Alone with the text

The outline rail is the only chrome beside the article.
